READY TO GET STARTED?

Choose your biometric method

Follow the steps below. For more details, read the Quick Start Guide.

#FACEIT

Facial Recognition

By recording the spatial geometry of the face — works by using a photo ID of the customer, previously stored in the system, and scanning their face for a match.

#PRINTPAY

Fingerprint + Palm

Multi-modal options for customer choice. Both fingerprint and palm authentication linked to a secure vault. Accommodates users who prefer not to use facial recognition.

#BLINKLINK

PayEye Iris

PayEye scans your iris to make contactless payments. The unique patterns in your iris provide one of the most accurate biometric identifiers available.

#SPEAKIT

Voice Recognition

Using vocal characteristics to identify individuals using a pass-phrase. Your voice is as unique as your fingerprint — secure and convenient.
